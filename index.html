About the Awards

Championing Workplace Wellbeing Since 1974

The ISMAUK Stress Management Awards celebrate individuals and organisations making a genuine difference to mental health and wellbeing at work. Now in their third year, these awards shine a light on outstanding practice across all sectors.

Who can apply?

The awards are open to individuals and organisations of any size, across any sector — public, private, or third sector. You do not need to be an ISMAUK member to enter.

How are entries judged?

A panel of independent judges from ISMAUK assesses all entries against published criteria. Scoring is based on evidence of impact, innovation, and commitment to sustained wellbeing.

When will winners be announced?

Shortlisted applicants will be contacted in October 2026. Winners will be announced at the ISMAUK Annual Awards event in November 2026.

What does winning mean?

Award winners receive a certificate, digital badge, press release support, and recognition across ISMAUK's communications channels and social media.

Award Categories

Choose Your Level

Three award tiers recognise different stages of stress management and wellbeing practice — from those just starting out to organisations with embedded, strategic programmes.

Bronze Award
Starting Out
20 marks

For individuals or organisations who are beginning their journey and can demonstrate early commitment, initial actions, and positive intent in workplace wellbeing.

  • 5 application questions
  • Evidence of initial steps taken
  • Open to individuals & organisations
  • No minimum timeframe required
Silver Award
Established Practice
25 marks

For those with an established and evidence-based approach to stress management, showing sustained effort and measurable outcomes over time.

  • 6 application questions
  • Evidence of sustained practice
  • Measurable outcomes required
  • Open to individuals & organisations
Gold Award
Strategic Excellence
45 marks

For those demonstrating exceptional, organisation-wide commitment to psychological safety and wellbeing, embedded in strategy, culture, and leadership.

  • 10 application questions
  • Strategic, board-level commitment
  • Comprehensive evidence required
  • Open to individuals & organisations

⭐ Special Recognition Certificate

In addition to the three main award tiers, the judges may award a Special Recognition Certificate to any nominee or applicant who demonstrates exceptional contribution, innovation, or personal impact — even if not selected for a main award. This is at the sole discretion of the judging panel.
